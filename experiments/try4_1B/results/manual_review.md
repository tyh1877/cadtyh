# Try-4.1B contact-sheet review

This is a development diagnostic and is not used as the independent topology GT.

- **R02_P04:** B0 is an elongated block with one tall cover. B1 visibly introduces
  a broad stepped cover, mounting holes, side recess and proximal boss. B2 changes
  to a compound low profile with separate transition and side-wall layers; it is
  visibly closest to the GT of the three, although the front/rear profiles and
  cover outline remain simplified. It no longer reads primarily as a short block.
- **R02_P06:** B1 makes carriage, rail, slider, jaw, linkage, pivot and gap bodies
  explicit. B2 adds jaw adapters and profile-shaped jaws/linkages. The mechanism
  is more articulated than B0, but layout, jaw orientation, linkage placement and
  dense carriage detail remain far from GT. Boxes/bars are reduced but still
  visually dominant.
