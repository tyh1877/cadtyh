# Robot A engineering text

PincherX-100 is a compact serial educational manipulator with a rotating base,
shoulder and elbow pitch joints, a wrist pitch joint and a compact two-finger end
structure. Coarse reconstruction should preserve the base, rounded joint housing
regions, upper-arm and forearm load paths, wrist block and opposed fingers. Small
holes, cosmetic details and hidden drive components are outside Try-5A.

The URDF is authoritative for link topology, joint frames, axes and limits. Images
inform broad envelopes, thickness, curved housing appearance and coarse body
families only.
