# Try-6.0-C1-A1 — L04 Kinematic Metric Prior Ablation: Phase-0 Gate

**Decision: `NOANCHOR_BASELINE_CONTAMINATED`.** The required paired A1-N/A1-F optimization was **not run**. The existing frozen C1-v2 F0, CAD compiler and visual registration contain the exact J03–J04 metric relation intended for removal. Under the user-frozen protocol, calling a condition with those shared inputs “NoAnchor” would invalidate the causal comparison. No benefit or harm of the anchor has been estimated by A1.

## Exact metric dependency chain

The sanitized URDF J04 origin is `0.063 m`, yielding the 63 mm J03–J04 relation. The L04 coarse planner converts child-joint origins to millimetres and stores **`interface_span_mm=major_length_mm=63`** in `LinkCoarseSpec`; the round3-verified F0 IR retains both at 63 mm despite its body scale factor 0.72. The actual frozen F0 FCStd reopens as one valid solid (9698.134 mm³; x bbox −11.5 to 65 mm). Its full x bbox is **not** itself 63 mm because interfaces extend beyond the joint span; nevertheless its construction uses the exact 63 mm distal joint placement. Every C1 candidate opens that F0 as `FrozenScaffold` and fuses it into `RigidGroup`. This is a direct A1-N leakage path, not merely contextual metadata.

The same value appears in the frozen functional backbone/KFDG distal port (`x=63 mm`), the fixed functional parameter spec, and FreeCAD compiler: the builder rejects an anchor other than 63 mm and places `TransitionEnd` at that metric x-coordinate. An unchanged compiler and scaffold therefore cannot produce a genuine no-anchor candidate, even if the URDF file is withheld from the condition's prompt.

C1-v2's registration is also anchor-derived: the right visible span **335 px / 63 mm = 5.31746 px/mm** and top visible span **322 px / 63 mm = 5.11111 px/mm**. The historical 12–52 mm ROI, 15/30/45 mm profile stations, visible widths in mm, projected contour coordinates, and profile-error division by `self.anchor` all inherit this value. Reusing cached registration, ROI or objective targets in A1-N would silently reintroduce the prior. Raw image/color masks and the frozen appearance slots (main housing present, pocket absent, transition present) are separable from the scale conversion.

The six body theta initial values and their mm bounds do not, by themselves, prove a direct arithmetic copy of 63 mm. They remain absolute physical-scale priors and would need explicit treatment in a valid future paired design. The older `wrist_block` width template (16 mm times a dimensionless visual factor) is a partial non-kinematic width gauge, **not** an existing clean longitudinal F0/scaffold or distal-frame gauge. Replacing the 63 mm F0/compiler/port after observing these inputs would change the frozen shared CAD pathway. A latent global scale without an independent camera calibration is also not an identified absolute metric scale under the current approximate orthographic registration; it was not implemented or resolved using GT/URDF.

## Gate outcome and scope

Allowed functional context could retain link identity, joint-axis directions, interface ownership/topology and appearance slots, but not the exact J04 translation, distal-port x, F0 geometry or 63 mm compiler/registration chain. The proposed anchor-independent image-domain objective remains **unfrozen and unrun** because the earlier F0/compiler gate failed. Neither A1-N nor A1-F proposed or selected a candidate; no CAD candidate, pre-GT lock, dimensional result, engineering-coordinate metric or normalized-shape metric was fabricated. The historical Direct-Qwen and C1-v2 results were not rerun or used as a surrogate paired comparison.

The 20-item technical checklist records **17 Phase-0 audit passes** and **3 paired-evaluation checks not run because of the provenance gate**. The independent validator passed **19/19 evidence-integrity checks**; that validates the fail-closed decision, not a completed anchor ablation. VLM calls, GT evaluations, KFDE calls, final 96-case mechanics, and formal-holdout evaluations are all 0. Holdout remains `accessed=false`, `evaluation_count=0`; C1-v2 and D1-v3 frozen manifests remain unchanged.

**Not supported:** any A1-F versus A1-N theta/loss comparison; a kinematic-anchor benefit or adverse effect; per-dimension errors; engineering IoU/Chamfer/HD95; normalized-shape fidelity; L03/L07 or robot-level generalization; mechanical/manufacturing performance. The immediate next step is **method/scale-protocol diagnosis before expansion**—specifically, independently pre-registering a clean shared scale gauge or a genuinely identifiable latent-scale treatment and verifying that F0/compiler, registration and ROI no longer leak 63 mm. This report does not implement that redesign.
