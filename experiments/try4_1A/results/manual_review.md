# Try-4.1A contact-sheet review

This current-agent review is a development diagnostic and is not used as a main
metric or independent judge.

- **R02_P02:** A0 incorrectly uses two full-length parallel members. A1 changes
  the CAD to one central web, an end junction and two terminal prongs. A2 retains
  that topology and adds rounded prong ends. The main topology error is removed;
  cover steps and detailed end housings remain simplified.
- **R02_P04:** A0 is a narrow tapered proxy. A1 adds a broad main housing, raised
  step, side channel and round joint region, visibly moving toward GT. A2 selects
  `stepped_housing` but produces an overly short/blocky body and loses A1's better
  proportions. Shape-family selection is plausible; parameterization/translation
  is poor.
- **R02_P05:** A0 contains two straight tapered rods. A1 expresses a carrier that
  branches to two prongs with a gap, but the prongs remain straight. A2 produces a
  compact U/fork profile with a slot and flange; its curvature and flange placement
  are still crude, but it is structurally and geometrically the largest improvement.
- **R02_P06:** A0 is an abstract box/rail/jaw assembly. A1 explicitly adds two
  rails, two jaws, links and pivots. A2 selects rail-slider and jaw-linkage families,
  yet the CAD remains a sparse collection of boxes and bars, far from the dense GT
  mechanism. This part remains the clearest failure.
