# T1 manual contact-sheet review

Scope: qualitative inspection of the 12 DEV_A/B T1 contact sheets. These notes
are diagnostic observations, not Phase-5 geometry metrics or Quality Gate scores.

## DEV_A

- **R01_P00:** the annular top and pedestal hierarchy are visible, but the result
  remains close to the coarse T0 base. Rear/base proportions, scallops and
  exterior transitions are weak.
- **R01_P01:** the top inset is now visible. The housing is still mostly a box and
  pivot-cylinder abstraction, with limited contour recovery.
- **R01_P02:** the open fork and side slots appear, but the orientation and
  proportions diverge strongly from the reference. The two plates read as long
  hanging members rather than the reference arm structure.
- **R01_P03:** a central opening and side slots are visible. Overall layout and
  plate profile remain coarse.
- **R01_P04:** the bracket slot, two distinct jaws and grip gap are retained. The
  bracket, jaw contour and linkage layout remain substantially simplified.

## DEV_B

- **R02_P00:** the generic pedestal is complete and editable, but the reference
  scalloped and polygonal exterior detail is largely absent.
- **R02_P01:** two supports and the central disk are separated correctly. Their
  profiles, spacing and surrounding housing differ from the reference.
- **R02_P02:** the generated tapered body and terminal cylinder do not reproduce
  the dominant open-frame/fork silhouette.
- **R02_P03:** taper, circular housing and a large cut are present. The fork/web
  topology and detailed transitions are not recovered.
- **R02_P04:** the offset/tapered body, channel and two representative holes are
  present. The exterior is considerably simpler than the reference.
- **R02_P05:** the roll cylinder, two fork arms and open gap are distinct. Arm
  contours and local transitions remain coarse.
- **R02_P06:** separate jaws, carriage and grip gap are preserved. Webs, rails,
  linkage members and local joint geometry are mostly absent.

## Interpretation

T1 improves feature bookkeeping and makes several missing T0 details explicit in
geometry, especially slots, openings, separate supports and jaw gaps. The main
failure remains visual grounding of silhouette, proportions and spatial layout.
The recipe vocabulary also encourages repeated box/cylinder/taper abstractions.
Technical completion therefore exceeds visual fidelity: all files are usable and
parametric, while several parts still have large reference-shape discrepancies.
