# Pre-Phase-9 v2 pilot manual review

- **R01_P00:** the v2 polygon footprint, tapered polygonal pedestal and annular
  seat are substantially closer than T1/T2 v1. The rear enclosure, scallops and
  local transitions remain incomplete, so Engineering Exterior LOD does not pass.
- **R02_P02:** v2 corrects the dominant topology from two full-length bars to one
  central web with a terminal fork and proximal pivot. End housings, cover steps,
  fork profile and local panels remain simplified. This is a real topology
  improvement but not a complete visible-detail pass.
- **R02_P05:** v2 changes the two straight rods into one compact forked carrier
  with a roll feature and opening. Its plate curvature, flange orientation,
  terminal notches and slot placement remain inaccurate; numeric Gate still fails.
- **R02_P06:** v2 exposes carriage, rails, jaws, links and pivots as separate
  bodies, but their layout and proportions do not resemble the dense GT gripper
  mechanism sufficiently. Topology improvement is not established.

Pilot decision: **FAIL**. Three parts show useful structural progress, but the
gripper criterion and full visible-topology requirement are not met. Do not start
the 12-part formal v2 matrix or Phase 9.
