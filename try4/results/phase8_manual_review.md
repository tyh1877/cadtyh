# Phase 8 accepted-artifact manual review

The 12 GT/T1/T2 isometric contact sheets were inspected after the formal run.
No accepted candidate meets the intended Engineering Exterior Reconstruction LOD.

- R01 bases and housings remain loft/box/cylinder abstractions; scallops, stepped
  enclosures and transitions are missing.
- R01_P02/P03 retain paired-bar layouts with poor correspondence to the reference
  carrier and horizontal forearm/fork topology.
- R01_P04 still omits most linkage, carriage and bracket profile detail.
- R02_P00 remains a generic pedestal rather than the polygonal/scalloped base.
- R02_P01, R02_P02 and R02_P04 demonstrate numeric false positives: volume and
  distance overlap can pass while the integrated housing, central-web topology or
  stepped cover structure remains wrong.
- R02_P03 still lacks the distal fork/web structure; R02_P05 uses straight tapered
  arms instead of curved slotted fork plates; R02_P06 omits most linkage and web
  topology.

Accordingly, numeric PASS alone is not reported as T2 success. The combined Gate C
requires post-review success; all 12 finish UNRESOLVED after the allowed budget.
