# Try-4 semantic mechanical reasoning

State the Macro-Part's exterior function, primary load path, proximal/distal
regions, joint housings, attachment boundaries, moving constituents and visible
working spaces. Use sanitized joints as kinematic context, not calibrated render
coordinates. For grippers and frames, say which constituents must remain separate.

Classify evidence-supported features as primary, functional, structural, surface
or interface. Do not infer internal motors, reducers, bearings, fasteners or exact
fits. Identify which geometry may be simplified and which semantic requirements
would be destroyed by fusion, filling an opening, or replacing the shape family.
